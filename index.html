Neurogenetics · Department of Neurology, Universitätsklinikum

From the genome to the neuron, and back to the clinic.

My research interests lie in the genetics of neurodegenerative disease, with a current focus on amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and frontotemporal dementia (FTD): understanding the genetic causes of disease, and their translational impact on the design of better therapeutics.

Research

What I work on

Understanding the genetic causes of neurodegeneration, and what they mean for patients and therapies.

01

Genetics of neurodegenerative disease

Understanding the genetic causes of neurodegenerative disorders, currently amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and frontotemporal dementia (FTD), using genome sequencing and variant interpretation.

  • ALS · FTD
  • Genome sequencing
  • Variant interpretation
02

Genetic biomarkers

Genetic markers that help characterise patients and disease, and support better-informed clinical research.

  • Patient stratification
  • Clinical genomics
  • Biomarkers
03

From genetics to therapeutics

Using genetic findings to inform the design of better therapies, including RNA-targeted approaches.

  • Translational research
  • RNA-targeted therapies
  • Antisense oligonucleotides
04

Genomics across populations

Most genetic studies of neurodegeneration come from European cohorts. My earlier work on Parkinson’s disease in India showed the value of studying under-represented populations.

  • Population diversity
  • Whole-genome sequencing
  • Parkinson’s disease
